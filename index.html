About BrightPen

BrightPen Academy is a growing learning centre focused on handwriting, abacus, and tuition up to Class 10. We provide structured training for students at different levels, with lessons shaped around each student’s pace, strengths, and areas for improvement. As a new academy, our focus is simple: friendly teaching, personal attention, and meaningful progress for every learner.

Personal teaching. Real progress.

BrightPen was created with a simple belief: students learn best when they feel comfortable asking questions, practicing at their own level, and receiving patient guidance. As the founder and teacher, every learner receives direct attention and a structured plan suited to their needs.
